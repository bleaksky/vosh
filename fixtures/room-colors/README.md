# Room colors fixtures

Hand written and synthetic. Nothing here was captured from a live session. Every line is game text the Aabahran server prints, built from its own format strings and area files, which live in the forsaken_lands source and the `area` folder beside it. Tolliver, Maren and Orla are invented names for players.

## looks.json

Room looks, each as a list of events in wire order, some with two looks in a row. A `gmcp` event is a packet, a `line` event is one line of text with the ANSI codes the server sends, and a `prompt` event is a prompt the server ends with a GA. `room` marks the lines a Room trigger matches, the armies, the things and the people a look lists after its exits line. `target` on a case is what you gave `tar` before the look, and on a line it marks the line of that person, which a Your target trigger matches. `src-tauri/src/session/tests/room.rs` plays each look through the session's own steps, and `crates/automation/src/trigger/engine.rs` highlights each word of each line in place and matches each line in the Text and Starts with modes, copied with and without its leading spaces.

Where the lines come from.

- The look itself, in order, is `do_look` in act_info.c. The room's name, two spaces and its description, a blank line, the exits line, then the armies, the things and the people. `do_look` sends Room.Info, Room.Chars and Room.Items right after the people. Up to d50e4a24 `gmcp_send` wrote each packet to the socket at once while the text waited for the end of the pulse, so the packets came before the look's text, as most cases have them. Since d50e4a24 the game queues each packet with the text, so it comes after the people and before anything else the pulse prints, as the cases that say so have them.
- A move is `move_char` in act_move.c and an immortal's goto is `do_goto` in act_wiz.c. Both show the room through `do_look` with "auto", so their looks have the same order. An immortal with holylight sees ` [Room %d]` after the room's name. The move and goto cases walk west from room 6910 into room 6909 and goto from room 5279 to room 6909, each in both orders. Where the packets follow the look, your target has its line on the first look all the same, since the session reads the Room.Chars after the people before they show, or colors the line again in place when that packet comes a read later. `src-tauri/src/session/tests/room.rs` plays each look in one read and with its packets a read after its people.
- Room 6910 in area/eastroad.are, Nearing the Crossroads, carries the `` `# `` code and the 256 color tint `do_look` gives a desert, and its description shows as the area file wraps it. Room 6909 has exits `D0` to `D3`, so its line reads `[Exits: north east south west]`.
- The armies are `show_room_armies` in armies.c, which prints each one to a player outside an area cabal as its long text and the reset code of two backticks, so each army line ends in `ESC[0;0m`. No army in the area files holds a color code, so that reset is the only code in the line. `A mighty Fortress looms over the area.` is Knight Fortress, army 12 in area/limbo.are, and `A horde of Demons hovers overhead, their talons dripping blood.` is a Demon Horde, army 18.
- The Crossroads is room 6909 in area/eastroad.are and Thickening Woods room 6904, each name with its color code after the 256 color tint `do_look` gives a field and a forest. Their descriptions show as the area file wraps them, the way `send_wrapped` sends them before the game learns your width.
- `A stand of blue dried leaves grows in the wild here.` is a sickle node from `assign_gather_node` in skills3.c, `%s grows in the wild here.` with `a stand of blue dried leaves`. Its Room.Items entry has the type `plant`.
- Mob 6904 resets in room 6909 as `A young werebeast stands here, leaning on his spear.` and mob 6936 in room 6904 as `A large murder of crows nearly turns the trees black here.`. Their Room.Chars names are `a werebeast` and `a murder of crows`. The werebeast's GREET program runs after the look in the same pulse. `A werebeast looks into the sky.` is its emote, and its two says in Beastial are `$n says in %s` from languages.c as a listener who knows Beastial hears them, the text in `` `# `` bold yellow.
- The stacked look puts three of object 5202 and one of 5201 in room 5279. Room.Items names each object once, in the order of the floor, and `list_to_char` puts the three gauntlets on one line with `( 3) `. A sleeping player prints as `Maren is sleeping here.`, with no title.
- A person in a fight prints with the short text or the name, ` is here, fighting ` and the one they fight, as in `A villager is here, fighting Maren.` and `Maren is here, fighting a villager.`.
- The exits line is `do_exits` with `auto` in act_info.c. Room 5279 in area/fortblac.are, The Bank of Aabahran, has one exit, `D2`, so its line reads `[Exits: south]`.
- The room name carries the `` `8 `` code area/fortblac.are gives it, which the server sends as `ESC[0;1;30m`, and the reset code after it, two backticks, as `ESC[0;0m`. The first look also has the 256 color tint `do_look` puts before the name of an inside room.
- The things are `list_to_char` in act_info.c, five spaces or a count like `( 2) ` before an object's long text. Objects 5201 and 5202 in area/fortblac.are give `A black-steel helm is here, gleaming darkly.` and `A pair of black-steel gauntlets rests on the ground.`. The inventory lines use their short texts, `a black-steel helm` and `a pair of black-steel gauntlets`, as `do_inventory` lists them under `You are carrying:`.
- The people are `char_to_char` in act_info.c. Mobs 5287 and 5283 reset in room 5279 and print their long texts, `A Blackwatch villager scurries about, taking care of business.` and `A representative of the Bank of Aabahran is here, ready to take your gold.`. Their Room.Chars names are their short texts, `a villager` and `a bank representative`. A resting player prints as `Tolliver is resting here.`, here with the `[AFK] ` tag whose AFK the server colors with `` `1 ``.
- `tar 3` sets your target to the third Room.Chars name in full. In the stacked look that is `a bank representative`, whose long text words the name another way.
- Room 4811 in area/gastride.are, Entrance to Fortress Hallway, has exits `D0`, `D2` and `D3`, so its line reads `[Exits: north south west]`. Mobs 4801, 4808 and 4812 reset there in that order, and `char_to_room` in handler.c puts each new one first in the room, so mob 4812 comes first. They print `A warrior medic stands here ready to tend to the wounded warriors.`, `A master warrior stands here watching over others.` and `A warrior stands here ready to train.`, and their Room.Chars names are `a warrior medic`, `a master warrior` and `a warrior`. `tar warrior` points at the first name that holds warrior, the medic, as `kill warrior` does in the game.
- `You have explored a quarter of The Eastern Road.` is `explore_room` in explore.c, in `` `8 `` bold black, which `move_char` in act_move.c runs right after the look. Room 6910 in area/eastroad.are, Nearing the Crossroads, has exits `D1` and `D3` and no mob or object resets there.
- `Maren walks in.` and `Orla walks in.` are `$n walks in.` in act_move.c, an arrival that lands after a look in the same pulse.
- `The day has begun.` is `weather_update` in update.c at hour 6.
- `You spot some fresh spur.` is `show_tracks` in skills4.c, which follows the exits line when the game draws its minimap.
- `It is pitch black ... ` is the dark room line in `do_look`, which prints no exits line.
- The prompt is the one `prompt all` sets, `%n%P%C<%hhp %mm %vmv> `, with sample numbers.

## lines.json

Single lines for the Room, time, and weather colors preset. `trigger` names the trigger that colors a line and `match` the text its color covers. A line with no `trigger` is a near miss that no trigger of the preset may touch. `src/automation/presets.test.ts` runs the preset's patterns on each one, `src-tauri/src/session/tests/room.rs` runs each through the session's own steps, and `crates/automation/src/trigger/engine.rs` highlights each word of each line in place, draws each weather line on every ground in `fixtures/readable/grounds.json`, and holds each near miss to the bytes the game sent.

- The exits lines are `do_exits` with `auto`. Room 5233, The Eastern Square, has exits `D0` to `D3`. Room 5279 has `D2`. Room 5200, Rock Bottom, has `D4` and a door at `D5` that resets closed, which the line shows in parentheses. The same room shows `(+down)` when you see a trap on that door, the `+` in `` `! `` bold red. A room with no exit you can see reads `[Exits: none]`, the same text the builder tutorial mob in area/higher.are echoes.
- The eleven time of day lines are `weather_update` in update.c, the five usual ones and the six it sends in eternal darkness.
- The WiZNET lines are `wiznet` in act_wiz.c, `` `&W`8i`&ZNET`8 ``, the time cut from ctime, and the message. The messages are `TICK!` from update.c, `Newbie alert!  $N sighted.` from comm.c and `$N has posted a note.` from recycle.c. Three more carry colors of their own, which the preset leaves on them. `` `!Corrupted Pfile detected: %s`` `` from comm.c is bold red, `` `&%s attacked %s at %d`` `` from `m_yell` in magic.c is bold white, here in room 5279, and `` `@%s has been forced wizinvis for idling > 13 ticks.`` `` from update.c is bold green.
- The says, tells and yells are the formats in languages.c and act_comm.c, `$n says`, `%s tells you` and `$n yells`, each with its color code around the text. The game has no gossip channel, so the newbie channel from `do_newbiechat` stands in for one. Each quotes a line the preset colors.
- `Welcome to Wiznet!` is `do_wiznet` in act_wiz.c.
- The prompts are the one `prompt all` sets, and `%e` alone from `do_promptexit` in act_info.c, which prints single letters, `---` while you are blind and `???` in forest mist.
- `Obvious exits:` and its row are `do_exits` typed by hand, and `You can't tell where the exits are.` is what it prints in forest mist.
- The room names are rooms 5279, 5233 and 5200 in area/fortblac.are, with their own color codes.
- The fourteen sky lines are `sky_event_text` in update.c, which `weather_update` sends to each player awake outdoors when the sky over the region changes, each in `` `& `` bold white. Whether a change brings rain, sleet or snow, and hail or a blizzard in a storm, follows the temperature where you stand, below 30, from 30 to 35, or 36 and up. A sky line can follow a time of day message in the same send.
- The ten room weather lines are `weather_affect_room` in update.c, which tells each player awake in an outdoor room when its blizzard, sandstorm, ice, mud or fog sets in or clears. The game sends them with no color.
- The weather near misses are a say and a tell that quote a weather line, the report `do_weather` in act_info.c prints when you type `weather`, `The sky seems a bit too clear.` from a thunder storm spell in magic2.c, and two lines `weather_affect_room` sends beside a change, the fog costing you your hiding and lightning striking someone in the room.

## preset.json

The triggers of the Room, time, and weather colors preset, exactly as `presetTriggers` in `src/automation/presets.ts` makes them. `src/automation/presets.test.ts` holds the two equal, so a change to the preset changes this file in the same commit, and the Rust tests install these triggers.
