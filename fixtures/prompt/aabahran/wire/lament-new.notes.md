Synthetic. The fake Aabahran in the test kit wrote it as case `lament-new` of `crates/prompt/src/testkit/wire.rs`. It is no socat capture, and it stays marked synthetic until James approves a capture to take its place. Run `VOSH_WRITE_WIRE=1 cargo test -p vosh-prompt --test wire` to write it again after a change to the fake.

The new build answers `lament` while you tank. Lamented tears lands, and Char.Affects goes out at once with an empty list and `"hidden":true`.

The prompt time packages follow, each one the song empties marked `"hidden":true`. Char.Combat keeps the guard's name and the `tank` object with your name and no `hp_pct`. The battle line is gone. The text prompt prints `Tester: ` alone on the tank line, since `%P` prints nothing under the song, then `[0/0hp 0/0mn 0/0mv]`.

Vosh hides your vitals, the tank's health, the opponent, your affects and your group. Once the session has had a Char.Prompt, the flags alone decide it.
