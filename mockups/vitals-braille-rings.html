vitals · braille concentric arcs

same concentric-arc geometry as the SVG version, but the dots are real braille — each character cell is a 2×4 dot patch, and the renderer lights only the dots that fall inside one of the three ring annuli AND within that ring's lit arc (clockwise from top, scaled by the vital's value). three text layers overlap at the same position, one per vital; since the three rings live at different radii, the dots never collide so colors stay pure with no blend mode. shown at three sizes so you can see how it scales.

      how the rendering works: each cell carries 2×4 dot positions. the algorithm
      walks every dot, computes its radius from the ring's center, and lights it if it falls inside
      that vital's annulus. arc fill: angle from top (12 o'clock), clockwise, compared against
      value × 2π. three layers: hp / mn / mv each render their full
      grid with only their ring's dots lit. layered at the same position with
      position: absolute; since the rings have different radii, no two layers ever
      light a dot at the same pixel.
