three independent canvases stacked on top of each other. each lane carries its own gradient area fill in the vital color, current value + min/max for the visible window on the right, and a pulsing live-beat dot at the rightmost edge. graphs scroll left every 200ms (5 samples per second, 120 samples kept = 24 seconds of history visible). easiest to read at a glance because each vital owns its own horizontal slice.

all three vitals on a single tall canvas. percent-normalized so a 0% hp and a 0% mana land at the same y-coordinate; the eye can spot correlations directly (mana dropped while hp climbed = healer cast cure light). lines glow with a soft drop shadow in the vital color. denser to read than stacked but better when you want to see resource trades over time.

data model: 120 samples kept per vital (24s of history at 5 samples/s). new samples enter at the right and shift the history one column left. percent (0..100) is the canvas y-axis so each lane uses its full vertical range regardless of the vital's max. the scenario buttons swap which random-walk distribution drives the simulated MUD ticks so you can see how the graph reads under different play states. for the live build the same sampler would subscribe to Char.Vitals GMCP and store its 120-sample circular buffer instead of generating random walks.
