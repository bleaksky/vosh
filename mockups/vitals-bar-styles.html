The numeric and percent columns follow. ramped uses 1/8-step partial blocks on the boundary cell so the bar moves at sub-character resolution (notice how a 51.2% bar reads differently from a 50% bar in ramped, but identically in solid). track is a CSS-rendered smooth bar — no glyph dependency, always proportional.
