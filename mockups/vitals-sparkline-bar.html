same width as the current bar. the empty cells (the portion you've lost) are filled with the sparkline of how you got here — most-recent sample at the right edge, oldest at the bar boundary. as the vital drops, more history is revealed; at 100% the sparkline disappears entirely (because you don't need to look at history when you're fine). no extra space.

why a sparkline here: the bar tells you state, the sparkline tells you derivative. in combat that is the difference between "the healer is keeping up" and "i'm about to die in three seconds." each variant keeps the bar as the headline (your eye finds it first) and lets the sparkline answer follow-up questions only when something feels off. colors match vosh's built-in ramps (mn blue, mv amber, hp green) so they fit beside the scrollback rather than competing with it.
