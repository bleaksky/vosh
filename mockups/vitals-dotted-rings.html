vitals · dotted arc rings

same concentric-arc geometry as before, but the strokes are dotted instead of solid. SVG, not braille — that gives perfectly round arcs while keeping the chunky character-grid texture you're after. three styles below: round dots tight, round dots loose, square (pixel-style) dots. all driven by the same simulator.

scenario
a · round · tight
hp 750 /1000 mn 150 /300 mv 50 /200

round dots at tight spacing (4px between dot centers). reads almost continuous but you can still see the discrete dot texture up close.

b · round · loose
hp 750 /1000 mn 150 /300 mv 50 /200

round dots, wider gaps (~7px between). each dot is its own visible mark; reads as a ring made of beads. this is closest to the chunky braille feel.

c · square · pixel
hp 750 /1000 mn 150 /300 mv 50 /200

square dots, pixel-art look. stroke-linecap: butt with short dash + gap gives the chunky terminal-grid feel without literally being braille.

how it works: each ring is an SVG <path> arc from 0° (top) to value × 360°. the dotted look is just stroke-dasharray with very short dashes. round dots → stroke-linecap: round with dash length 0. square dots → stroke-linecap: butt with a small square dash length. a dim track arc behind (the full circle at low opacity) keeps the ring shape visible when the live arc hasn't filled it. no font issues — pure SVG.
