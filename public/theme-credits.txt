Vosh theme credits

Every built in theme in Vosh names where its colors come from, who made
them, and the license they carry. You find the same in Settings under
Appearance, beneath the theme gallery. The MIT license asks that its
notice travel with every copy, so each MIT notice below is copied whole
from the license file of its source, at the address beside it.

Vosh itself is free software under the GNU General Public License
version 3. The full text ships with the Vosh source code in LICENSE.
